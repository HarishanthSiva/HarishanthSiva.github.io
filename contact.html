How to Find Us

We are located in Hatton — Sri Lanka's scenic hill country, famous for its tea estates and cool mountain air.

The Elite Peak is situated on Dimbulla Road, Hatton — easily accessible from the town centre. Look for our sign on the 1st Lane off Dimbulla Rd.
