About The Elite Peak

Villa and Family Restaurant · Hatton, Sri Lanka

Who We Are

Where the Mountains
Meet the Table

Nestled in the lush, misty hills of Hatton — Sri Lanka's celebrated tea country — The Elite Peak is more than a restaurant. It is an experience. Founded with a singular vision of bringing together the finest flavours of Sri Lanka, South India, and beyond, we have built a place where every guest is treated like family.

Our kitchen is led by chefs who understand that great food is not just about ingredients — it is about heart. Whether you are sharing a weekend Biryani Sawaan with five loved ones, indulging in our signature Chef's Special Kili Parotta, or simply enjoying a quiet evening with a glass of fresh Avocado Juice and the mountain air, we are here to make every moment extraordinary.

"A Symphony of Taste, Elegance, and Service."

The Elite Peak is a villa and family restaurant, meaning we cater not only to casual diners but also to families and groups looking for a full dining experience. Our outdoor seating lets guests breathe in the cool air of Hatton while savouring authentic, lovingly prepared dishes.

We are proud of our 4.7-star rating on Google — a reflection of the trust, warmth, and quality our guests experience every single visit.
