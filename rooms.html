A Retreat Above the Clouds

Nestled in the cool highlands of Hatton at over 1,100 metres, The Elite Peak Villa offers four vintage-style rooms where warm hospitality meets mountain serenity. Each room is thoughtfully appointed with comfortable beds, hot water bathing, and the quiet charm of a bygone era.

Wake to crisp highland air, dine on our celebrated cuisine just steps away, and let the Dimbulla valley unfold before you.
